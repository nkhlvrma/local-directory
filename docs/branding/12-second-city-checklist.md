# Second-City Checklist

A concrete checklist for evaluating and launching the second-city pilot — deliberately written as a gate to pass, not a calendar to follow, since expanding before Lucknow itself is proven would repeat the same mistake at larger scale. Cross-reference with the Week 12–13 "neighborhood expansion rehearsal" in `08-90-day-content-calendar.md` and `07-acquisition-playbooks.md` — the second-city decision should be made using real data from that rehearsal, not from this checklist in isolation.

Note: the codebase already has a second city configured in `src/lib/site.ts` (`bangalore`, alongside `lucknow`, in `CITY_MAPS`) — that's infrastructure readiness, not market readiness. This checklist is about the latter.

---

## Gate 0: Is Lucknow actually ready to be left partially unattended?

Before evaluating a second city at all, confirm the first one doesn't still need the founder's full attention:

- [ ] The supply-recruitment playbook (`07-acquisition-playbooks.md`) has been run at least once as a *repeatable* weekly process, not just as an initial launch push — confirmed via the Phase 3 "systemize" checklist.
- [ ] The north-star metric (`11-measurement-plan.md`) is being tracked and reviewed at least weekly, and shows a stable or improving trend over the most recent 4+ weeks, not a one-time spike from launch novelty.
- [ ] Contact-checked listing share is holding near 100% without constant manual chasing — i.e., the freshness habit has become routine, not a special effort.
- [ ] There's no open, unresolved trust issue (a spike in report rate, a batch of stale/dead listings) that a second city would distract from fixing.

**If any of these aren't true, the honest answer is "not yet" — expanding to a second city while Lucknow is still fragile risks both cities being under-resourced by one founder.**

---

## Supply access

- [ ] Does the founder (or someone they can bring in) have a real, personal way into the second city's business community — family, friends, a former colleague, anyone who isn't a cold start? The single biggest lever in Lucknow's Phase 1 was in-person visits and warm connector introductions, not cold outreach — a second city with zero personal access will be materially harder, not just "the same playbook again."
- [ ] Is the target category mix (whichever categories worked best in Lucknow — likely electricians, tiffin/food, tutors, salons, or whatever the 90-day data actually showed) present and reachable in the second city, or does the market skew differently (e.g. more organized retail, fewer small independent shops)?
- [ ] Are small businesses in the target city genuinely WhatsApp-native for customer contact, the way they are in Lucknow? This isn't safe to assume nationally — verify with a handful of real conversations before committing, since the entire product mechanic depends on it.
- [ ] Realistic estimate: how many weeks of in-person/phone outreach would it take to reach the same 40–60 verified-listing baseline Lucknow reached in its first month? If the honest estimate is meaningfully longer (e.g. because there's no local network to lean on), factor that into the timeline rather than assuming Phase 1 will repeat at the same speed.

## Local partner identification

- [ ] Identified at least 2–3 candidate neighborhood-connector-equivalents in the second city *before* launch — the same RWA/local-shop-owner/group-admin profile described in `07-acquisition-playbooks.md`, not invented from scratch after arriving.
- [ ] If the founder can't personally spend significant time in the second city, identified a specific local person (paid, volunteer, or otherwise incentivized — decide deliberately, don't default to unpaid) who can do the in-person recruitment work that a remote founder can't. Running the in-person outreach script from `10-business-outreach-scripts.md` remotely (phone-only) is a real degradation from what worked in Lucknow — plan for that gap explicitly rather than hoping it won't matter.
- [ ] Confirmed this local partner (if one exists) actually understands and will hold the trust rules — no overclaiming "Contact checked," no accepting payment for inclusion, no shortcuts on the reachability check, and explicit owner consent before publishing a number. A partner who doesn't internalize this can damage trust in a way that's hard to walk back.

## Category demand validation

- [ ] Ran a small, cheap validation before committing real resources: e.g. a version of the "seeding practical questions in local groups" tactic (`07-acquisition-playbooks.md`) tried in a second-city-specific WhatsApp/Facebook group, to see if the same kind of "does anyone know a reliable X who actually replies" question gets real engagement there.
- [ ] Confirmed (even informally) that residents in the second city have the same underlying pain point Lucknow validated — dead numbers, unreachable listings on Google/Justdial, WhatsApp-based informal commerce — rather than assuming the problem is universal without checking.

## What "ready to expand" looks like — the actual go/no-go signal

Expansion should be triggered by evidence, not a calendar date (the plan's "one second-city pilot later" is directionally right but shouldn't collapse into "after 90 days, expand regardless of what the 90 days showed"). Concretely, "ready" means:

- [ ] Lucknow's north-star metric and supporting metrics (`11-measurement-plan.md`) have been stable for at least 4–6 consecutive weeks, not trending in a single early spike.
- [ ] The supply-recruitment playbook has been proven repeatable by someone other than pure founder heroics at least once — e.g. the Week 12 neighborhood-expansion rehearsal within Lucknow itself succeeded at a comparable pace to the original launch, showing the playbook generalizes to a new area without needing to be reinvented.
- [ ] A real answer exists to "who does the in-person work in the second city" — not a placeholder assumption that the founder will simply also be there.
- [ ] The founder has an honest read on whether splitting attention across two cities will materially slow Lucknow's own growth, and has decided that trade-off is worth it — this should be a deliberate choice, not an unexamined default.

**If these aren't all true, the right move is not "launch anyway, learn as we go" — it's to keep deepening Lucknow (more categories, more neighborhoods, per the Phase 3 playbook) until they are.** A second city started before the first is stable tends to produce two half-built directories instead of one strong one, which directly undermines the trust promise the whole brand is built on.
