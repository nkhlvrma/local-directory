# 90-Day Content Calendar

Week-by-week, not just phase-by-phase, per the source plan's phases (1–30 supply, 31–60 resident usage, 61–90 repeatability). Each week names: what gets posted/sent, in which channel, and the goal of that week. This calendar assumes the founder is the sole executor — volume is deliberately kept realistic for one person, not padded to look comprehensive.

Channels used: **WhatsApp groups** (community/society/local groups the founder is a genuine member of, plus 1:1 outreach), **WhatsApp Status** (founder's own number, or a Karib business number once one exists), **Instagram** (feed + Stories).

---

## Phase 1 — Supply (Weeks 1–4)

### Week 1
- **Goal:** build the target business list and finalize outreach materials — no public content yet.
- **WhatsApp groups:** none (not ready — posting before there's anything to show wastes the community's attention).
- **WhatsApp Status:** none.
- **Instagram:** none. (Optional: reserve the handle/username now even if unused.)

### Week 2
- **Goal:** first wave of business visits; get to 10 live verified listings.
- **WhatsApp groups:** none yet publicly — any WhatsApp activity this week is 1:1 outreach to businesses, not group posting.
- **WhatsApp Status:** none.
- **Instagram:** none.

### Week 3
- **Goal:** recruit 3–5 neighborhood connectors; use their intros to add more listings.
- **WhatsApp groups:** none public-facing yet — connector conversations are 1:1.
- **WhatsApp Status:** none.
- **Instagram:** none.

### Week 4
- **Goal:** consolidate coverage to 40–60 listings; prepare the share card and soft-launch materials for Phase 2.
- **WhatsApp groups:** none yet.
- **WhatsApp Status:** none.
- **Instagram:** create the account if not already done; no posts yet, just set up (bio, profile photo, link).

*Why nothing public in Phase 1:* posting to a community before there's real, checkable substance behind it (a working directory with real verified listings) risks a first impression that Karib is vaporware or an idea, not a working product — worse for long-term trust than staying quiet for four weeks.

## Phase 2 — Resident usage (Weeks 5–8)

### Week 5 — Soft launch
- **Goal:** get the founder's own network to try one real search and report back; fix what breaks.
- **WhatsApp groups:** 1:1 personal messages (not group posts) to ~10–15 people in the founder's own building/society/friend network, asking them to try one search.
- **WhatsApp Status:** one soft post — "Been building something the last month, would love a second pair of eyes: [link]" (personal, not promotional tone).
- **Instagram:** none yet — still fixing issues surfaced by soft-launch feedback.

### Week 6 — First community seeding
- **Goal:** be genuinely useful in 2–3 local groups; light trust-building.
- **WhatsApp groups:** post 2 of the practical-question-style posts from `07-acquisition-playbooks.md` (in different groups, spaced across the week, not the same day).
- **WhatsApp Status:** 2 posts — one listing spotlight ("Found a great tiffin service in [neighborhood], verified their WhatsApp — search 'tiffin' on Karib"), one behind-the-scenes ("Spent today walking [neighborhood] adding shops to Karib").
- **Instagram:** first post — an introduction post explaining what Karib is and why it exists (the positioning statement from `02-positioning-and-messaging.md`, adapted to a short caption).

### Week 7 — Share card push
- **Goal:** get the "verified local businesses" share card genuinely forwarded, not just posted once.
- **WhatsApp groups:** post the share card (from `07-acquisition-playbooks.md`) in 2–3 relevant groups, framed as a resource, not an announcement. Follow 1 more practical-question post.
- **WhatsApp Status:** 3 posts — 2 listing spotlights, 1 "here's how Karib checks a number is real" trust post (ties to the Verified badge explainer copy in `06-verification-community-copy.md`).
- **Instagram:** 2 posts — one listing spotlight (a real business, real photo if available, real "Verified" and would-recommend framing), one "Verified means..." trust-explainer post.

### Week 8 — Diagnose and fix the weakest funnel step
- **Goal:** identify where residents drop off (search → view → WhatsApp click) using early data from `11-measurement-plan.md`, and address it directly.
- **WhatsApp groups:** 1 post only this week — deliberately lighter, since founder time goes into fixing the identified funnel issue instead.
- **WhatsApp Status:** 2 posts, business-as-usual cadence.
- **Instagram:** 1 post, tied to whatever the funnel fix was (e.g. if trust was the blocker, another Verified-explainer post from a different angle; if discoverability was the blocker, a "how to search Karib" tip post).

## Phase 3 — Repeatability (Weeks 9–13)

### Week 9 — Document and prune
- **Goal:** identify which channel is actually working; cut the rest.
- **WhatsApp groups:** continue at whatever cadence week 6–8 showed was sustainable and effective (likely 1–2 posts/week) — this week's real work is measurement, not new content.
- **WhatsApp Status:** maintain 2–3/week cadence if it's been the strongest channel so far (WhatsApp Status is typically the lowest-effort, most-native channel for this audience — expect it to outperform Instagram for this specific product).
- **Instagram:** 1 post — pause deeper investment here until week 9's review confirms it's worth the founder's time relative to WhatsApp Status.

### Week 10 — Systemize the winning supply channel
- **Goal:** turn ad hoc connector/visit recruitment into a repeatable weekly block.
- **WhatsApp groups:** 1–2 posts, business as usual.
- **WhatsApp Status:** 2–3 posts, including one aimed at business recruitment this time (e.g. "If you run a shop in [neighborhood] and want to be listed, message me") rather than only resident-facing content — Phase 3 should start blending supply and demand content instead of treating them as separate phases.
- **Instagram:** 1 post, a "how it's going" milestone post (real numbers: "X verified listings across Y neighborhoods now") — milestone posts work well for social proof once there's a real number worth sharing.

### Week 11 — Deepen existing neighborhoods
- **Goal:** add 1–2 new categories to existing neighborhoods; re-run the share card with better numbers behind it.
- **WhatsApp groups:** re-post the share card (updated with current listing count) in the same groups from week 7 — the second push should land better now that there's visible growth to point to.
- **WhatsApp Status:** 2–3 posts, mix of new-category spotlights and a repeat trust post.
- **Instagram:** 1 post spotlighting the new categories added.

### Week 12 — Neighborhood expansion rehearsal
- **Goal:** run a compressed version of the full playbook in one new Lucknow neighborhood, as a rehearsal for eventual second-city expansion.
- **WhatsApp groups:** identify and join 1–2 groups specific to the new neighborhood; 1 practical-question post there by week's end.
- **WhatsApp Status:** 2–3 posts, at least 1 specifically about the new neighborhood.
- **Instagram:** 1 post introducing the new neighborhood's early listings.

### Week 13 — Review and decide
- **Goal:** consolidate 90 days of data and content performance into a clear view of what worked, to feed `12-second-city-checklist.md`.
- **WhatsApp groups:** maintain steady-state cadence (1–2/week) — no new experiments this week, this week is for review.
- **WhatsApp Status:** maintain steady-state cadence (2–3/week).
- **Instagram:** 1 milestone/recap post summarizing the first 90 days — genuinely useful as both a resident-facing trust signal ("here's what we built in 90 days") and an internal record.

## Cadence summary (for quick reference)

| Weeks | WhatsApp groups | WhatsApp Status | Instagram |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–4 (supply) | 0 public posts (1:1 outreach only) | 0 | 0 (account setup only, wk4) |
| 5–8 (resident usage) | 0 → 1–3/week, ramping | 1 → 2–3/week, ramping | 0 → 1–2/week, ramping |
| 9–13 (repeatability) | 1–2/week steady | 2–3/week steady | 1/week steady |

**Note on realism:** this calendar assumes roughly 3–5 hours/week of founder time for content and community activity on top of the supply-recruitment work in `07-acquisition-playbooks.md`. If that time isn't actually available most weeks, cut Instagram before cutting WhatsApp Status or WhatsApp groups — for this specific audience and product, WhatsApp-native channels are doing more of the real work than a polished Instagram presence would.
